The conference made this effect visible. Portfolio executives were learning from other operators’ transformations. Each successful deployment strengthens a provider’s position in the next decision.

03
The competition

Competitors are already winning the transformation budget.

The threat is visible in the business workflows being deployed, the delivery partners around them and the access providers already have to Blackstone’s senior leaders.

Anthropic / business workflow momentum

Claude is winning transformational use cases.

Claude featured in most of the Applied AI presentations I attended, tied to business transformation and significant ROI ambitions. Blackstone’s relationship with Ode provides another delivery route across the portfolio.

Why it matters: governance concerns raised by CTOs are not stopping adoption. Each production win builds business familiarity and a reference for the next company.
Microsoft / embedded distribution

The installed base can become the default AI choice.

The estimate shared with me was that roughly 70% of portfolio companies use Azure. Copilot Studio and Foundry came up repeatedly, supported by infrastructure, relationships and familiar tools already in place.

Why it matters: Microsoft can enter the AI decision through an existing operating relationship. The Azure estimate is conversation based, not a verified portfolio census.
The wider competitive picture and PE coverage models

Access and ease of delivery

AWS Bedrock and AgentCore were visible in agentic foundations. Google and Salesforce have strong executive relationships. TextQL appeals to teams looking for a straightforward way to deploy conversational intelligence.

Dedicated PE divisions and programs

Providers I spoke with described quota carrying PE leads focused on revenue and consumption, and partnership leads focused on sponsor relationships. Both models had a clear executive remit across the portfolio and recognition for the business they help generate.

We risk retaining the data workload while another provider becomes the transformation partner the business calls first.

The opportunity window narrows as customers establish workflows, skills and delivery relationships. Full business transformation remains a delivery bottleneck, which gives us a concrete way to compete now.

04
The opportunity

Make Databricks the platform behind complete business processes.

The discussion that resonated with CTOs and Blackstone executives was how to make each AI investment improve the way the enterprise operates: shared business context, governed data and decisions that move into action.

Claude and Copilot are already winning business workflows and have enterprise integration and governance capabilities. Our opportunity is to establish Databricks as the reusable foundation connecting data, analytics, models, agents and operational applications, including solutions that use those interfaces. CTOs agreed that a strong platform can protect future investment and help avoid new technology debt from disconnected agents, duplicated business logic and fragmented controls.

Select a portfolio example to explore the workflow.

BME EUROPE

Connect the full purchasing cycle to margin and control.

Opportunity discussed

Bring purchasing, supplier data, invoice validation and approvals into one coordinated process. Extend the same foundation into supplier onboarding, contract compliance and performance management.

CONNECTPurchases, supplier records, contracts and invoices
DECIDEMatch transactions, identify exceptions and apply business rules
ACTRoute approvals and reconcile permitted actions with the source systems

Business impact to quantify

Cost per invoice, manual touches, exception cycle time, duplicate payments and supplier leakage. Working capital effects should be measured separately from EBITDA.

What can repeat

A purchasing and supplier operations pattern that can be adapted to other companies’ ERPs, policies and approval structures.

ACC · REVANTAGE · BRE · BROADER REAL ESTATE

Turn revenue intelligence into operating decisions.

Opportunity discussed

Connect existing revenue platforms and optimization models with financial, asset and operational data. Build central intelligence that moves a recommendation through approval, execution and feedback, while preserving effective existing models.

CONNECTRevenue models, leases, asset data, financials and operations
DECIDEEvaluate pricing, occupancy, concessions and capital priorities
ACTRoute recommendations to operators and measure the resulting outcome

Business impact to quantify

NOI lift, revenue per available unit, vacancy and concession reduction, capital returns and decision time. These are proposed measurement areas, not reported gains.

What can repeat

A shared decision infrastructure across real estate operators, with local models, asset economics and operating controls.

SAFE HARBOR MARINAS

Build intelligence from acquisition through daily operations.

Opportunity discussed

Revenue and utilization improvement, time to integrate an acquisition, integration cost and time to realize operating synergies.

CROSS INDUSTRY PATTERN

Connect contractual obligations to revenue and collection.

Replicable opportunity

Link contracts, delivered services, invoices and cash. Detect missed billing and discrepancies, apply contract rules, and route corrective actions through the right approvals.

Contract intelligence, reconciliation and approval components across services, infrastructure, real estate and other portfolio companies.
